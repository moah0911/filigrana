# The quiet cost of asking a machine to be sure

There are lots of reasons to be careful about certainty, and several ways to fail at it.
We talked about it for about an hour, and afterward he went toward the station, gray and
silent. Although the enquiry had begun earlier, nobody had written anything down. Maybe
the point is obvious; however, few people said so, and she thought it somewhat cold.

Among the group, judgment varied. Acknowledgement came late, and the work went onwards
regardless. Frequently they met at this point in the winter, and eventually they agreed that
the report was confidential. In spite of the noise, the enquiry continued, and he didn't
recognise the color of the building when he returned.

He would rather leave than stay. That, at least, was certain, though the reason was not.
