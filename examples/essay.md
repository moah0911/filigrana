# The quiet cost of asking a machine to be sure

There are a lot of reasons to be careful about certainty, and several ways to fail at it.
We talked about it for about an hour, and afterwards he went toward the station, grey and
silent. Although the enquiry had begun earlier, nobody had written anything down. Perhaps
the point is obvious; however, few people said so, and she thought it somewhat cold.

Among the group, judgement varied. Acknowledgement came late, and the work went onward
regardless. Frequently they met at this point in the winter, and in the end they agreed that
the report was confidential. In spite of the noise, the enquiry continued, and he did not
recognise the colour of the building when he returned.

He would rather leave than stay. That, at least, was certain, although the reason was not.
